Spaghetti Bolognese

Are you feeling hungry? Well, with this dish, you are going to be resolved from your hunger problems by enjoying a delicious Spaghetti Bolognese. Which rich flavors and great texture will make you awe at how good it tastes.

Ingredients

  • Spaghetti
  • Beef
  • Beef Cube
  • Sugar
  • Worcestershire Sauce
  • Tomato Paste
  • Crushed Tomatoes
  • Red Wine
  • Onion
  • Garlic
  • Thyme
  • Bay Leaves

Steps:

  1. Saute garlic and onion - about 3 minutes
  2. Brown the beef - about 2 minutes.
  3. Add everything else
  4. Enjoy!
