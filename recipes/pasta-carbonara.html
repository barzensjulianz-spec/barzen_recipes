Pasta Carbonara

Would you want an eggy pasta for you and your family to enjoy that's fast, quick, and easy. Well, look no further than my version of Pasta Carbonara

Ingredients:

  • Bacon
  • Water
  • Garlic
  • Spaghetti or Linquine
  • Grated Parmesan
  • Eggs
  • Salt and Pepper
  • Fresh Parsley

Steps:

  1. Add bacon to a pan
  2. Continue simmering
  3. Remove bacon from pan
  4. Saute garlic
  6. Cook spaghetti or linquine
  7. Slowly poor hot water into the egg mixture
  8. Let pasta rest for a few minutes
  9. Enjoy!
